Key Takeaways

  • Always clarify requirements before designing
  • Consider scale, availability, and consistency tradeoffs
  • Justify your technology choices with concrete reasoning
  • Know when to use SQL vs NoSQL, cache vs no cache

Interview Tips

  • Draw the architecture diagram first, then explain components
  • Think out loud — interviewers value your reasoning process
  • Acknowledge what you don't know and explain how you'd find out
  • Ask clarifying questions: read vs write heavy? consistency requirements?
